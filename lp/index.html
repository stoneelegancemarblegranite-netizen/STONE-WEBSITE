Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a quartz countertop installation cost?

Our current quartz packages start at $1,755 installed for up to 35 sq ft, with the slab, fabrication and labor included. Final pricing depends on square footage, edge profile and cutouts — send your details and we return a written, itemised quote within one business day.

How long does countertop installation take?

Installation itself is a single day. From digital measurement to finished install, most projects are completed in 5 to 7 business days.

Do you remove old countertops?

Yes. Removal and disposal of your existing countertops is handled by our crew on install day, with floors and cabinets protected and a full cleanup before we leave.

Do you provide fabrication and installation?

Both. We fabricate in our own Clifton, NJ shop and install with our own insured crew — templating, cutting, edge polishing and installation stay with one team, so nothing gets subcontracted out.

What areas do you serve?

We serve New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania within roughly 50 miles of Clifton, NJ — including Passaic, Bergen, Essex, Hudson, Union and Morris counties, New York City, Rockland County and Eastern PA.

What does your warranty cover?

We provide a direct labor warranty covering structural seams, leveling, and installation craftsmanship.

Will the installation create a huge dust mess inside my home?

No. All major cutting, profiling, and polishing occur off-site at our Clifton facility. Installers protect your floors and clean up fully before leaving.

Are your installation crews insured?

Yes, our entire team is fully insured to protect your property at every stage.
