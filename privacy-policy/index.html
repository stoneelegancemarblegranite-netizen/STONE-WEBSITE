1. Who we are

Stone Elegance Marble & Granite is a countertop fabrication and installation company based in Clifton, New Jersey, serving customers across New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit an estimate request on this website, we collect the information you type into the form. Depending on the form, that includes:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your city or ZIP code
  • The stone type you are considering and the approximate square footage of the project
  • Anything you write in the message field

We also receive whatever you choose to tell us if you call us, text us, email us, or message us on Facebook or Instagram.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the time of the request. Where our analytics and advertising tags are active, they may also record pages viewed, the referring source, approximate location derived from your IP address and interactions such as form submissions.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and our forms do not accept, payment card numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive identifiers. Please do not send that kind of information through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare and send you the estimate you requested
  • To contact you about your project by phone, text or email
  • To schedule measurement, templating and installation visits
  • To answer questions and provide customer service
  • To measure how our website and advertising perform, so we can improve them
  • To keep records required for our business, and to comply with the law

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and the business, and only as needed for them to perform that role:

  • Web3Forms — processes our website form submissions and delivers them to our email inbox. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.
  • Google — Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads measure site and campaign performance; Google Maps powers the service-area map; Google Fonts serves the typefaces. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider — stores the website files and server logs.
  • Email provider — receives and stores the estimate requests sent to our inbox.

We may also disclose information if required by law, by a court order, or to protect our legal rights, and we may transfer information as part of a sale or reorganisation of the business.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

This website does not set advertising cookies of its own. Cookies and similar identifiers may be set by the third-party services listed above — chiefly Google Analytics and Google Ads, which we use to understand which pages and ads generate estimate requests, and to measure conversions. Google Maps may also set cookies when the embedded map loads.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and adjust personalised advertising at Google My Ad Center. Blocking cookies does not prevent you from using the site or requesting an estimate.

We currently do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, as no common standard for them has been adopted.

6. How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and project correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and to keep accurate business records — typically for the duration of the project plus the period required for warranty, accounting and legal purposes. Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of the relevant Google service. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, subject to records we are required to keep.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you about your project or with marketing messages

To make a request, email stoneelegancemarblegranite@gmail.com or call (862) 464-5987. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first. Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws — including New Jersey and California — may have additional rights under those laws, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined in those laws.

To stop receiving text messages or calls from us, simply tell us so in a reply or on the phone.

8. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted connection. We limit access to estimate requests to the people who need them to do the work. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold.

9. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults arranging home or commercial improvement work. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business or the services we use change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
