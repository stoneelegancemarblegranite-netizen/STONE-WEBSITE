3. Measurement and templating

Digital laser templating is performed once cabinets and supporting structures are installed, level and ready. Fabrication cannot begin before templating, because the template is what the stone is cut from. If cabinets are moved, replaced or adjusted after templating, a new template and additional charges may be required.

You are responsible for confirming appliance models, sink and faucet selections, and cooktop cutout requirements before fabrication begins. Cutouts are made to the specifications supplied.

4. Natural stone and material variation

Granite, marble and quartzite are natural products. Colour, veining, movement, pitting, fissures and mineral variation are inherent characteristics of natural stone, not defects. Samples, showroom pieces and photographs on this website — including our portfolio images — indicate the general character of a material, not the exact appearance of the slab that will be installed in your home.

Engineered quartz offers greater colour consistency but can still vary between production batches. Wherever practical, we invite customers to view and approve the actual slab before fabrication.

Seams are a normal part of stone fabrication on runs and islands that exceed slab dimensions. We plan seam placement to be as discreet as possible and join slabs with colour-matched resin, but a seam remains visible and tangible to some degree.
