The material

The intersection of luxury and strength

Quartzite is a 100% natural metamorphic rock formed deep within the earth when sandstone is subjected to extreme heat and pressure. This natural process fuses the quartz grains together, creating an incredibly dense, hard stone that frequently features the sweeping, elegant veining traditionally associated with marble.

  • Unmatched natural hardness

    Quartzite actually ranks higher on the Mohs scale of hardness than granite. It is highly resistant to scratching, chipping, and daily wear-and-tear, making it ideal for active culinary spaces.

  • The marble aesthetic without the compromise

    Many homeowners desire the soft white and grey veining of Carrara or Calacatta marble but want to avoid its fragility. Quartzite provides this exact aesthetic profile but with structural integrity meant for heavy use.

  • Heat & UV resistance

    Because it is formed by extreme geological heat, natural quartzite handles hot pots and pans exceptionally well. It is also UV resistant, meaning it will not fade in sun-drenched kitchens or outdoor patio applications.

  • Slab authenticity

    Because quartzite is a natural stone, no two slabs are exactly alike. Your kitchen island or bathroom vanity will feature a completely unique, organic geological pattern that cannot be replicated in a factory.

What is the difference between quartzite and quartz?+

This is the most common point of confusion. Quartzite is a 100% natural rock mined from the earth in blocks and cut into slabs. Engineered quartz is a man-made product created in a factory by mixing crushed stone with resin and pigments. Quartzite is highly heat-resistant but requires sealing; engineered quartz is non-porous (no sealing) but is sensitive to high heat.

Does quartzite require sealing?+

Yes. Like granite and all natural stones, quartzite is porous. We apply a professional-grade sealer upon installation to protect against liquid absorption and staining. You should reseal your quartzite countertops approximately once a year to maintain optimal stain resistance.

Will quartzite etch like marble?+

True quartzite will not etch from everyday kitchen acids (like lemon juice or vinegar) the way marble does. However, because stones are labeled somewhat loosely in the global supply chain, some "soft quartzites" share properties with marble. We source directly from trusted suppliers and verify the density of our slabs to ensure you get true quartzite durability.

How long does fabrication take after templating?+

Once you have selected your specific slab and our team has completed the digital laser templating of your cabinets, fabrication generally takes 5 to 7 business days. The actual on-site installation is completed in a single day.
