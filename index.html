How long does the custom countertop installation process take?+

Once slab selection and digital laser templating are complete, fabrication typically takes 5 to 7 business days. On-site installation is completed in a single day.

What geographical areas do you serve?+

We cover a 50-mile radius around our Clifton, NJ headquarters, including Passaic, Bergen, Essex, Hudson, Union, and Morris counties, as well as New York City, Rockland County, and Eastern Pennsylvania.

Are your installations covered by a warranty?+

Yes. Stone Elegance provides a dedicated craftsmanship warranty covering all fabrication and installation labor, in addition to individual manufacturer material warranties.

What is the main maintenance difference between Quartz and Granite?+

Quartz is non-porous and never requires sealing. Granite is a natural porous stone that requires periodic sealing (typically once per year) to maintain stain resistance.
